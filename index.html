SomaAlign: Reliability-Aware Multimodal Representation Learning for Soft Robotic Grasping

Overview

We introduce Soma, a synchronized multimodal dataset for soft robotic grasping, and SomaAlign, a reliability-aware framework for unified multimodal representation learning.

Soma: Synchronized Multimodal Grasping Dataset

Soma provides synchronized visual, tactile, inertial, depth, and semantic observations under two-finger and three-finger soft robotic grasping configurations.

Soma-Success: Grasp Outcome Evaluation

Soma-Success provides diverse grasping trials for evaluating grasp outcomes across different objects, gripper configurations, and failure modes.

Multimodal Understanding

SomaAlign connects heterogeneous physical observations with semantic understanding, enabling multimodal reasoning across different sensing modalities.

Cross-Modal Generation and Conclusion

The aligned multimodal representation enables cross-modal generation while preserving semantic consistency across heterogeneous sensing modalities.

Supplementary Information

Additional dataset statistics, parameter diagnostics, robustness results, downstream evaluations, cross-modal generation, multimodal question answering, and inference efficiency analysis.
